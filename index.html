Don’t ignore

Diabetic Foot Problems — Warning Signs

If you notice any of these, see a foot specialist early. Early detection means timely treatment and better recovery.

Diabetic Foot Ulcer

Open sores on the foot that need urgent specialist care.

Infections & Wounds

Wounds that are slow to heal or show signs of infection.

Corns, Callus & Cracks

Hardened skin and cracks that can turn into ulcers.

Foot Deformity

Changes in foot shape or toe deformity.

Poor Circulation & Numbness

Tingling, numbness, cold feet or colour changes.

Gangrene (severe cases)

Tissue damage needing immediate emergency care.

Awareness

Protect Your Feet, Protect Your Life

✓

Check your feet daily for cuts, blisters, redness or swelling.

✓

Wash your feet daily with lukewarm water & dry them gently.

✓

Moisturize to prevent dry skin and cracks — avoid between the toes.

✓

Wear comfortable, well-fitting footwear. Never walk barefoot.

✓

Keep your blood sugar under control. Avoid smoking & alcohol.

✓

Do not ignore foot pain or numbness — visit a specialist regularly.
