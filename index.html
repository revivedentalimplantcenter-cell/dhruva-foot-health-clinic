Warning signs of diabetic foot problems

If you notice any of these, see a foot specialist early. Early detection means timely treatment and better recovery.

  • Foot deformityChanges in foot shape or toe deformity.
  • Poor circulation and numbnessTingling, numbness, cold feet or colour changes.
  • Gangrene (severe cases)Tissue damage needing immediate emergency care.

Awareness

Protect your feet, protect your life

  • Check your feet daily for cuts, blisters, redness or swelling.
  • Wash your feet daily with lukewarm water and dry them gently.
  • Moisturise to prevent dry skin and cracks — avoid between the toes.
  • Wear comfortable, well-fitting footwear. Never walk barefoot.
  • Keep your blood sugar under control. Avoid smoking and alcohol.
  • Do not ignore foot pain or numbness — visit a specialist regularly.
